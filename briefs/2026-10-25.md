## Top Stories

### 1. Anthropic launches Claude Duet, a proactive background agent
**Source**: MacRumors / Thurrott | **Category**: Models

On Oct 21 Anthropic released Claude Duet in research preview: a standalone agent (iOS, Android, web) that monitors connected apps, sends a morning brief, works in its own VM and browser, and asks for approval before consequential actions such as sending messages, paying, signing, or deleting. It reportedly runs on Claude Opus 5.5 and is waitlist-only for US Pro and Max subscribers, with usage drawn from existing plan limits. It is Anthropic's entry into an agent race already joined by Meta's Muse (Sept 8) and OpenAI's Dots (Sept 29).

**Source**: Fox News (BBC interview) / Fast Company / CSMonitor | **Category**: Policy

Microsoft's Brad Smith is urging binding international AI rules: independent evaluations, mandatory incident reporting, and an "emergency brake" for rogue agents. Meanwhile the fight over federal preemption of state AI laws continues, and the administration is reportedly weighing pre-release evaluations for frontier models.
