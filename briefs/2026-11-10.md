### 2. Anthropic's November IPO roadshow faces investor doubts
**Source**: Reuters (via Let's Data Science); The Gadgeteer | **Category**: Industry

Reuters reports that potential investors doubt Anthropic can start its roadshow on schedule and still list before Thanksgiving. The November 2 S-1/A left the price-range fields blank. Reported valuation targets run from $1T–1.5T to about $2T. The same amended filing added a risk factor saying Anthropic "may imminently meet" its Responsible Scaling Policy threshold for "dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI." The filing also concedes the company has missed such speed-ups in the past, which is unusual language for a prospectus.

### 3. OpenAI closes $11B at a $1.25T valuation, below its target
**Source**: Reuters / Bloomberg (via The Star, GV Wire) | **Category**: Industry

OpenAI closed about $11 billion as the first portion of a round that may reach $20 billion. That is down from an earlier $30B+ goal, and the $1.25 trillion valuation is below the $1.4–1.5T it had sought. Together with Anthropic's IPO jitters, this is the clearest sign yet that frontier-lab valuations are now being negotiated downward rather than simply accepted.

### 4. Midterm results reshape the US AI policy outlook; Scott Wiener heads to Congress
**Source**: MIT Technology Review; Mind and Machine Weekly | **Category**: Policy

Democrats took both the House and the Senate in the November 3 midterms. Scott Wiener, the California state senator behind SB 1047 and SB 53, won Nancy Pelosi's San Francisco seat. His campaign drew more than $3M from Public First Action, a group with substantial Anthropic funding. Separately, voters reportedly approved 40 of 41 local ballot measures opposing data centers, which signals that the grid and land-use side of AI is turning into an electoral issue.

### 5. Fireworks AI security incident: disclosure details diverge
**Source**: The Neuron; NHI Mgmt Group; Digg (CEO Lin Qiao statement) | **Category**: Industry

Today's digests say Fireworks disclosed a cyberattack that exposed customer datasets and models. Fireworks' earlier October disclosure said something narrower: internal credentials were used to read evaluation-job environment variables, which probably exposed customer-held third-party API keys, with "no evidence" of access to models or datasets. If the new reporting is accurate, the scope has grown substantially. Inference-platform customers should rotate any secrets they stored with Fireworks.

### 6. China issues rules on AI agent identity ("passports for bots")
**Source**: Ctrl+AI+Reg (Techieray) | **Category**: Policy

China's telecoms ministry published a November 6 notice that governs identity and attribution for AI agents, and a second part on public-contract clauses is due in late November. It is one of the first national-level attempts to regulate agents as distinct actors rather than as features of the model behind them.

### 7. Thinking Machines Lab nears a $5B raise at $40B
**Source**: Business Insider (via Jawlah) | **Category**: Industry

Mira Murati's Thinking Machines is reportedly close to closing a $5 billion round at a $40 billion pre-money valuation, led by Accel. Even as the two largest labs see valuations pushed down, second-tier frontier labs are still raising mega-rounds.
