**Source**: Wilson Sonsini / Baker Botts | **Category**: Policy

It mandates AI security risk management programs, algorithmic impact assessments, and documented measures to prevent algorithmic discrimination. With federal legislation still stalled, Colorado joins California in defining the de facto national floor for AI governance — and companies operating in both states face materially different compliance obligations for the same systems.
