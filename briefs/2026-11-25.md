### 2. Anthropic Launches Claude Ship: Agents That Review, Fix and Approve PRs
**Source**: 9to5Mac / Decrypt / ITPro / Digital Trends / Technology.org / Yahoo Tech (TechCrunch syndication) | **Category**: Products

Anthropic launched Claude Ship on November 23. It bundles three GitHub agents: **Code Review** (generally available), **PR Steward** (beta, fixes issues on open PRs) and **Claude Approvals** (beta). Claude Approvals approves routine PRs only under a policy file written by each team. It is available to Team and Enterprise orgs, excluding those on Zero Data Retention, HIPAA configurations or customer-managed keys. Usage is billed by token, separately from plan usage. Eligible Enterprise orgs can claim at least $10,000 in credits by January 15, 2027. Anthropic recommends starting in observe-only mode and warns the agents may miss bugs or security vulnerabilities. Its internal figures (merged PRs "more than doubled" June→September, reverts down "more than 40%") are self-reported. **Correction:** yesterday's brief listed "Claude Ship" as unverified. It is real, and coverage appeared after that brief was compiled.
