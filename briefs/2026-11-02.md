### 2. Microsoft's Enforceable AI Privacy Terms for Schools Take Effect
**Source**: AP / Fortune / GovTech | **Category**: Policy

The national AI privacy and safety standard that Microsoft announced in September with the AFT and UFT teachers' unions took effect on November 1. It restricts Microsoft's use of student and educator data and gives districts contractual remedies, including cancellation and damages. Reporting differs on whether every district is covered automatically or must write the terms into its own agreement. Google was invited to sign on and has not publicly committed.

### 5. New York RAISE Act Registration Window Opens for Frontier Labs
**Source**: Center for Democracy & Technology / regulatory trackers | **Category**: Policy

This month, frontier AI developers must begin registering with New York State under the RAISE Act. Full compliance, including 72-hour safety-incident reporting, begins in January 2027. It is the first US state regime to put incident disclosure deadlines on frontier labs. It also lands the same week as the London World AI Regulation Summit (November 5-6), where regulators are expected to discuss EU AI Act enforcement and liability for agentic AI.
