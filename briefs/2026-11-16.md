## Top Stories

### 1. Nvidia heads into Tuesday's earnings with Kress as both CFO and interim CEO
**Source**: CNBC / The Motley Fool / NVIDIA Form 8-K | **Category**: Industry

Nvidia reports Q3 fiscal 2027 results after the close on November 17, with the call at 2 p.m. PT. It is the first report since Jensen Huang's resignation, which the board accepted on November 11 and announced on November 12. Nvidia guided to about $108B in revenue (±2%) and about 74% gross margin, down from 75% because of higher memory costs. Analysts are a little higher, at about $108.8–109B in revenue and $2.47 adjusted EPS. Colette Kress holds both the CFO and interim CEO jobs. Investors will want to hear about the Vera Rubin production ramp, which Kress said would begin this quarter, and who now owns the customer and partner relationships Huang handled himself.

### 2. Anthropic IPO: pricing Wednesday, trading Thursday, range still $70–$78
**Source**: CNBC / Quartz / Yahoo Finance | **Category**: Industry

The November 10 S-1/A covers 825 million Class A shares: 801.4M newly issued and 23.6M sold by existing holders. At the top of the range, Anthropic nets up to about $64.35B. Pricing is expected November 18, and ANT shares should start trading on Nasdaq November 19. The article itself says the price is still a range, so treat $78 as the top of the range, not a final price.

### 3. Axios: Lutnick stalled Trump-ordered Nvidia–China chip curbs
**Source**: Axios | **Category**: Policy

Axios reported on November 11 that Commerce Secretary Lutnick slowed export controls the White House had agreed to after Oval Office discussions. Treasury Secretary Bessent reportedly confronted him over Commerce's resistance, especially on Megaspeed. This matters more now that Huang's resignation has drawn Senate Banking Committee scrutiny of chip-export policy. China's access to Nvidia hardware is being decided inside the administration, and the departments involved disagree.

### 4. Fractile raises $720M at a $7B valuation for memory-first inference chips
**Source**: SiliconANGLE / The Next Web | **Category**: Industry

Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed and Redpoint led the London chipmaker's Series C. CapitalG, BlackRock, the UK Sovereign AI Venture Fund and the British Business Bank also invested. Fractile's design puts SRAM and compute on the same die, betting that memory bandwidth limits inference more than raw compute. Its valuation is up from about $1.1B in May. Its first chips are not due until 2027, and the reported ~$250M Anthropic chip deal has not been confirmed by either company. The round is a large pre-revenue bet that inference hardware is still an open market.

### 5. Gemini 4 Hydrogen comes to Search worldwide
**Source**: Google / The Neuron | **Category**: Models

Google has extended Gemini 4 Hydrogen, its mass-market Gemini 4 model, to AI Mode in Search for users worldwide. It uses an upgraded "query fan-out" that runs searches across a question's subtopics at the same time and returns structured comparisons. Reports disagree on whether general availability began November 12 or later. Either way, Google's newest model now sits in front of its largest audience.

### 6. Japan to publish a new Defense AI Vision by the end of November
**Source**: CASRAI | **Category**: Policy

Defense Minister Shinjiro Koizumi said on November 15 that the ministry will revise its July 2024 AI policy. The revision will stress human responsibility, operational use of data, and safe implementation. It follows Japan's earlier talk of binding orders for advanced civilian AI developers, and suggests Tokyo is now setting AI rules for both civilian developers and the military.

### 7. ChatGPT Wallet: a "coming soon" item in the app, not yet launched
**Source**: PCWorld / RuntimeWire | **Category**: Products

Weekend digests described a ChatGPT Wallet that lets agents pay without seeing card numbers, but there is no official OpenAI announcement yet. PCWorld found a "Coming soon" Wallet entry in the mobile app. RuntimeWire found launch copy in the desktop app's files, but only after bypassing the app's display checks. OpenAI is clearly building a reusable payment layer for agentic commerce on top of Instant Checkout. Wait for OpenAI's own announcement before treating launch dates or other details as fact.
