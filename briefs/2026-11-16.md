### 3. FAA ground stop puts the new SMART AI traffic tool under scrutiny
**Source**: GeekWire / Washington Times / KXAN | **Category**: Policy

The nationwide ground stop on Friday, November 13 came three days after the FAA extended its SMART AI planning tool to all 22 en route centers. Officials, including Transportation Secretary Duffy, blame aging telecom equipment and say SMART is an advisory tool not connected to core flight-tracking systems; DHS Secretary Mullin, however, says a possible AI role is still being investigated. Whatever the cause, it is the first high-profile test of public trust in AI inside safety-critical federal infrastructure.
