### 5. EU AI Act Reaches Full Applicability on August 2
**Source**: European Commission | **Category**: Policy

The EU AI Act's final provisions become fully applicable on August 2, 2026 — 32 days from today. Prohibited AI practices have been enforceable since February 2025; governance and transparency rules since August 2025. The August 2 deadline brings obligations for high-risk AI systems into force, with companies deploying AI in hiring, credit scoring, critical infrastructure, and law enforcement facing mandatory conformity assessments, documentation requirements, and human oversight provisions.

**Source**: Gunderson Dettmer / Verifywise | **Category**: Policy

Colorado's AI Act took effect June 30, requiring security risk management programs, bias impact assessments, and algorithmic discrimination protections. California's Transparency in Frontier AI Act separately requires developers of large frontier models to publish risk frameworks, disclose safety incidents, and implement whistleblower protections, with penalties up to $1 million per violation for companies exceeding $500M revenue. State-level AI compliance is no longer theoretical — enforcement posture is actively tightening.
