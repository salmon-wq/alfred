### 9. Google TurboQuant Targets KV Cache Bottleneck at ICLR 2026
**Source**: Crescendo AI / Crunchbase | **Category**: Research

Google's research team presented TurboQuant at ICLR 2026, addressing one of the most persistent inference bottlenecks in large language models: KV cache memory overhead. The algorithm combines PolarQuant vector rotation with Quantized Johnson-Lindenstrauss compression to achieve significant memory reduction without degrading output quality. KV cache size is the primary constraint limiting how long context windows can be held in GPU memory during high-throughput inference — reducing it has direct cost and latency implications at production scale.
