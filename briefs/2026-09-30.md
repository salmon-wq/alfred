## Top Stories

### 1. White House and Tech CEOs Sign Voluntary AI Safety Accord
**Source**: CNBC, Al Jazeera | **Category**: Policy

Leaders of major US tech companies signed an agreement at the White House on Tuesday to self-police AI development: internal safety controls, an outside auditor to test those controls, and internal boards to review the audit reports. President Trump called the pact "morally binding" and said he will appoint an adviser to oversee it within days. The accord reaffirms the administration's preference for voluntary commitments over statute and follows its UN General Assembly rejection of any "globalist scheme" to control AI.

### 2. Anthropic's Leaked IPO Prospectus: $42B Net Loss, $518B in Compute Commitments
**Source**: Fortune, CNN Business | **Category**: Industry

A leaked draft S-1 shows Anthropic's 2025 revenue grew roughly 12x to $4.6B, its operating loss widened to $8.06B, and its net loss reached about $42B, against $518B in future cloud and infrastructure obligations. Risk factors run to 80 pages, against 48 on the business itself, and state that its models could pose a "catastrophic or existential risk to humanity." The listing is reportedly expected after the November midterms, with valuation talk above $2T. It is the largest financial disclosure yet from a frontier lab.

### 3. OpenAI DevDay: "Dots" Agents, GPT-6.1 Sol, and a Model Pulled on Safety Grounds
**Source**: CNBC, BGR | **Category**: Models

OpenAI announced more than 20 products and features. The headline is "dots": always-on ChatGPT agents running on GPT-6 Astra, each with its own cloud computer and browser, that work toward user goals around the clock. OpenAI also released GPT-6.1 Sol, pitched as stronger at agentic coding and computer use, and plugin extensions that let developers build full applications inside ChatGPT. Notably, it shelved GPT-6.1 Astra because the model did not meet its safety standards, and cut the $200 Pro tier from 20x to 10x Plus usage.

### 5. Anthropic Launches Claude Sonnet 5.5
**Source**: VentureBeat, SiliconANGLE | **Category**: Models

Released Monday, a week after Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 keeps Sonnet 5's list price ($2/$10 per million input/output tokens) while running about 30% faster and cutting per-task cost by up to 30% through fewer tool calls. It scores 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0, above Opus 5.5's reported 66.4%. It is also the first Sonnet to ship with the cyber safeguards previously reserved for Anthropic's top models. ### 6. Claude Suffers Broad Outage Across App, Code, Cowork, and API

Anthropic reported elevated error rates across claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Cowork and the API starting 14:21 UTC on September 29. Coming a day after a flagship launch, the incident underlines how much developer workflow now depends on a single provider's uptime.

### 7. Congress Floats a Pause on Advanced AI Until a New Regulator Exists
**Source**: Nextgov/FCW | **Category**: Policy

Lawmakers introduced several AI measures this week, including joint legislation that would restrict development of more advanced AI tools until a dedicated regulatory agency is created. Other bills target AI-assisted cyberattacks. None is likely to pass soon, but together they mark a sharp contrast with the White House's voluntary-accord approach.
