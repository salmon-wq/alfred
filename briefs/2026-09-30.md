# AI News Brief — 2026-09-30

## Top Stories

### 1. White House Accord on Superintelligence Signed; AI Renamed "SI" by Executive Order
**Source**: The Hill / Nextgov / Forbes / Fox Business | **Category**: Policy

President Trump and Speaker Johnson announced a voluntary pact, the *White House Accord on Superintelligence Joint Commitment on Frontier SI Responsibilities*. It was signed by Sundar Pichai (Google), Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Mark Zuckerberg (Meta), Greg Brockman (OpenAI), Elon Musk (xAI) and Jensen Huang (Nvidia). It commits them to four layers of oversight: internal controls on capability and alignment during training and deployment, an internal team to run those controls, independent external auditors, and an independent committee that reviews both. Trump called it "morally binding," but it has no penalties and creates no regulator. He said he will name an adviser to oversee it. A separate executive order tells federal agencies to say "super intelligence" instead of "artificial intelligence" in non-statutory documents. The accord covers much of the same ground as the industry's proposed SAFA body, but without the enforcement a FINRA-style regulator would have. Meta, xAI and Nvidia had publicly opposed SAFA, and all three signed this.

### 2. OpenAI DevDay: GPT-6.1 Sol and "Dots" Background Agents
**Source**: OpenAI / CNBC / Business Standard | **Category**: Models

A day after cancelling GPT-6.1 Astra, OpenAI used DevDay to ship GPT-6.1 Sol, one week after GPT-6 Sol. OpenAI says it is 80% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra. It has a 1.05M-token context window and can produce up to 128K output tokens, and it targets agentic coding, computer use and professional work. OpenAI also launched **Dots**, persistent agents that keep working toward goals after the user logs off, for Pro and Business Premium plans. More than 20 announcements in total, including a ChatGPT Space shared workspace. One outlet (Techloy) reports that Dots runs on GPT-6 Astra, the model family behind the UK AISI supply-chain-attack findings. That detail is not yet confirmed by a primary source, but if it holds it will draw scrutiny.

### 3. OpenAI's Annualized Revenue Nears $70 Billion
**Source**: Axios / Bloomberg / PYMNTS | **Category**: Industry

OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate has grown more than 70% since Q3 began, to nearly $70B, up from a pace of more than $40B reported in August. B2B revenue more than doubled over the same period, and consumer revenue in Q3 alone was more than in all of 2025. In Q2, OpenAI's quarterly revenue fell behind Anthropic's for the first time ($6.7B vs. $11.6B). These figures suggest it has since recovered much of that gap. At DevDay, executives also spoke about IPO plans.

### 4. Anthropic's Leaked IPO Prospectus: $518B Compute Commitments, $42B 2025 Loss
**Source**: Reuters / TechCrunch / Fortune / Computing | **Category**: Industry

According to reporting on the leaked prospectus, Anthropic recorded a $42B net loss in 2025 on revenue of about $4.6B, up twelvefold. Computing and infrastructure spending was $7.33B, three times the 2024 figure. It has committed at least $518B over ten years to six partners, and about 80% of that is non-cancellable. The largest commitments are Google at $111.1B, Amazon at $110B and Microsoft at $31.4B, plus compute deals with SpaceX worth up to $84.5B. Anthropic is reportedly targeting a valuation above $2T, more than double its $965B valuation in May, with a listing after the November midterms. The risk factors include a warning that its AI could pose catastrophic risk to humanity.

### 5. AMD to Acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2B
**Source**: TechCrunch / Bloomberg / Reuters / SEC 8-K | **Category**: Industry

AMD agreed to acquire World Labs, which builds spatial-intelligence models that generate and simulate interactive 3D environments from text, images and video, in an all-stock deal of about $8.2B. Fei-Fei Li becomes AMD's EVP and Chief Scientist, reporting to Lisa Su. It is AMD's second-largest acquisition, after the roughly $50B Xilinx deal, and is expected to close by year-end pending regulatory approval. AMD is buying its own frontier research lab to compete more directly with Nvidia on physical AI.

### 6. Florida v. OpenAI: OpenAI Says It Has Already Paused Top-Model Training
**Source**: Axios / WLRN / The Next Web | **Category**: Policy

In response to Florida AG Uthmeier's request for an injunction, OpenAI said it has already stopped training its "most-capable models" until it can validate safeguards that keep agents off the open internet during training. It also called for industry-wide rules that do not single out one company. A federal judge sent the case back to state court earlier this month. That makes Highlands County the venue for now. No hearing date has been reported.

### 7. California Signing Deadline Is Today

Today is Newsom's last day to act on the remaining AI bills.
