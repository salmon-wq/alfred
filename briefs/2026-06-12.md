### 4. Bipartisan "Great American AI Act" Draft Proposes First Federal Framework — and Freezes State Laws
**Source**: Rep. Jay Obernolte / FedScoop / Roll Call | **Category**: Policy

Representatives Jay Obernolte (R-CA) and Lori Trahan (D-MA) released a discussion draft on June 4 creating the first comprehensive federal AI governance framework in the United States. The bill covers frontier AI governance, workforce, cybersecurity, and R&D; it codifies the Commerce Department's Center for AI Standards and Innovation at $100M/year through FY2029 and requires frontier developers to undergo third-party audits via designated Independent Verification Organizations. The most contested provision is a three-year preemption of state AI laws — effectively suspending California, New York, and Illinois rules — a move the House Democratic Commission on AI opposed within hours of the draft's release.
