## Overview
On a quiet US Thanksgiving news day, regulation leads the AI news: Congress wants to see how much AI is building AI, New York bans chatbot toys, and states guard Character.AI users' chat logs during its bankruptcy sale.

## Top Stories

### 1. Bipartisan House Bill Would Require Reports on AI-Built Models
**Source**: The National Desk / TokenPost | **Category**: Policy

Reps. Josh Gottheimer (D-N.J.) and Nathaniel Moran (R-Texas) introduced a bill on Nov 25 that would require large AI developers to file quarterly reports. The reports would say how much AI contributes to building newer models and how much human review is left. The bill does not restrict autonomous AI research. It is the first federal proposal aimed specifically at recursive self-improvement, which until now has been mainly a concern of safety researchers.

### 2. New York Bans AI Chatbot Toys for Five Years
**Source**: NBC News | **Category**: Policy

Gov. Kathy Hochul signed a five-year ban on making and selling toys with built-in AI chatbots. The ban takes effect in February, and New York is the second state after California to pass one. Separately, AG Letitia James has set up a pre-enforcement unit ahead of the state's AI safety law, which takes effect Jan 1. New York is now one of the most active state AI regulators.

### 3. States Press for Privacy Safeguards in Character.AI Bankruptcy Sale
**Source**: NY Attorney General / Bloomberg Government | **Category**: Policy

About 40 attorneys general (reported as 39 states plus D.C.) want any buyer of Character Technologies' assets to accept limits on how users' private chat logs are used. They note that many of those logs likely came from minors. The proposed schedule set the auction for Nov 23 and the sale hearing for Nov 24, but no outcome had been reported as of this brief. The result will be an early test of whether conversations with a chatbot can be sold as an ordinary business asset.

### 4. OpenAI Begins Unifying ChatGPT Chat and Work
**Source**: The Neuron (digest) | **Category**: Industry

OpenAI has started rolling out a combined Chat and Work experience to a small group of ChatGPT Pro subscribers. The rollout includes shared usage limits, so heavy users may reach their caps sooner. It follows the mixed reception of GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on Nov 17. OpenAI is now pushing consumers and agentic workloads onto one quota.

### 5. Amazon Reportedly Close to Buying Decart for More Than $7B
**Source**: Calcalist / Globes (via FourWeekMBA) | **Category**: Industry

Calcalist reports that Amazon is close to a deal for Decart worth more than $7B in cash, stock, and deferred payments. Annapurna Labs chief Nafea Bshara is reportedly leading the deal, which suggests Amazon wants Decart's real-time video models and inference optimization for its Trainium chips. Neither company has confirmed terms.
