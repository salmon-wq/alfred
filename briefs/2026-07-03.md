**Source**: BuildFastWithAI / The Neuron | **Category**: Policy

### 3. EU AI Act Full Compliance Deadline: T-30 Days
**Source**: European Commission / Gunderson Dettmer | **Category**: Policy

The EU AI Act becomes fully applicable on August 2, 2026 — 30 days from today. Companies operating in Europe that deploy high-risk AI systems must have completed conformity assessments, technical documentation, and transparency disclosures by that date. Fines for non-compliance reach €35M or 7% of global annual turnover for prohibited-practice violations. With the summer deadline now imminent, legal teams across the EU and US are in final-sprint mode.
