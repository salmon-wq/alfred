## Top Stories

### 1. Fable 5 Restored After Historic 19-Day Government Suspension
**Source**: BuildFastWithAI / The Neuron | **Category**: Policy / Models

Anthropic's Fable 5 flagship returned to all users worldwide on July 1 at 3:31 pm ET, after the US Department of Commerce lifted the emergency export-control order it had imposed on June 12 — the first government-ordered suspension of a commercial AI model in history. The ban was triggered when Amazon researchers discovered a jailbreak that caused Fable 5 to identify and demonstrate exploitation of a software vulnerability. The DoC lifted controls after independent testing confirmed that Opus 4.8, GPT-5.5, and Kimi K2.7 could all reproduce the same exploit, eliminating any unique-offensive-capability rationale. The model returned with new classifier safeguards and a proposed jailbreak-severity scoring framework that may become an industry standard.

**Source**: MIT Technology Review, TechTimes | **Category**: Models / Industry

Jumper's departure is both a talent blow to DeepMind's scientific AI credibility and a signal that Anthropic is positioning itself to own the life sciences vertical.

### 4. EU AI Act Digital Omnibus Receives Final Council Approval
**Source**: Council of the EU, DLA Piper | **Category**: Policy

On June 29, the Council of the EU gave final approval to the **Digital AI Omnibus** — the first formal amendments to the EU AI Act since its June 2024 adoption. The headline change: high-risk AI system obligations (Annex III) are deferred **16 months**, from August 2, 2026 to December 2, 2027. Critically, **Article 50 transparency duties are not deferred** and remain live on August 2 — companies deploying AI-generated content face live disclosure and labeling requirements in less than 30 days. The SME compliance threshold was also expanded to companies up to 750 employees and €150M in revenue.
