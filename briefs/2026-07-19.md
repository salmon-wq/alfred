### 4. Illinois Signs First US Law Requiring Independent Audits of Frontier AI
**Source**: Bloomberg Law / StateScoop | **Category**: Policy

Governor JB Pritzker signed SB 315, the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act, on July 6 — making Illinois the first state to mandate annual independent third-party audits for frontier model developers with over $500 million in annual revenue. The law requires catastrophic-risk evaluation, safety incident reporting within 72 hours, and accurate public disclosures. OpenAI endorsed the bill. Requirements take effect January 1, 2028, and the law applies to any model trained on more than 10²⁶ compute operations — a threshold that currently captures a handful of frontier labs.

### 5. EU AI Act Enforcement Arrives August 2 — With a Significant Amendment
**Source**: Technology.org / ComplianceHub.Wiki | **Category**: Policy

August 2 brings GPAI model enforcement and Article 50 transparency obligations into full effect: providers must disclose AI interactions to users, mark synthetic content in machine-readable format, and label deepfakes. However, the Digital Omnibus on AI, signed July 8 and awaiting publication in the EU Official Journal, extends Annex III high-risk AI systems (covering recruitment, credit scoring, law enforcement, and education tools) from August 2026 to December 2027 — a seventeen-month reprieve. Medical device and safety-product AI moves further still, to August 2028.

### 6. China's AI Agent Regulations Take Effect July 15
**Source**: AI Governance Institute / Inside Global Tech | **Category**: Policy

China's Implementation Opinions on Intelligent Agents became enforceable on July 15, establishing the world's first dedicated regulatory category for AI agents as distinct from general AI models. The framework covers accountability chains, disclosure requirements, and usage restrictions specific to agentic behavior — ahead of any comparable framework in the US or EU. This positions China as the rule-setter for a product category all major labs are actively building.
