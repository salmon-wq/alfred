### 6. White House Releases National AI Policy Framework
**Source**: Consumer Finance Monitor / Holland & Knight | **Category**: Policy

The framework also calls for privacy protections and age-verification requirements for AI services accessible to children. The preemption recommendation is already drawing opposition from states with active AI legislation (California, Colorado); the battle over federal vs. state jurisdiction looks set to define U.S. AI governance for the next two years.
