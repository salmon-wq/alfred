### 1. OpenAI GPT-5.6 Goes Public After Government Security Hold

### 2. Anthropic Finds a "Global Workspace" Inside Claude
**Source**: VentureBeat, ExplainX | **Category**: Research

Anthropic published breakthrough interpretability research on July 6 introducing "J-space" — a small set of privileged neural patterns inside Claude where concepts are held for silent internal reasoning, readable without appearing in model output. The accompanying "J-lens" method (Jacobian-based) enables real-time reading and steering of this workspace without modifying the model. Neuroscientists Stanislas Dehaene and Lionel Naccache confirmed meaningful parallels with global neuronal workspace theory, the leading cognitive science account of conscious access in humans — though Anthropic explicitly stops short of claiming consciousness.

### 3. Illinois Signs the Nation's Strongest State AI Safety Law
**Source**: Capitol News Illinois, NBC Chicago, WTTW | **Category**: Policy

Governor JB Pritzker signed SB 315, the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act, on July 6. The law targets companies with at least $500M in annual revenue, mandating annual independent safety audits, published safety plans, confidential whistleblower channels, and employee protections for those raising safety concerns — effective January 1, 2027. Advocates describe it as surpassing comparable California and New York frameworks; it passed with bipartisan support and signals that state-level frontier AI regulation is accelerating independent of federal action.
