**Source**: NPR / White House | **Category**: Policy

The Trump administration released an executive order on June 2 directing federal agencies to slash regulatory burdens on AI development while asking companies to voluntarily submit powerful new models for government review up to 30 days before public release. The order explicitly frames safety as collaborative rather than compulsory — a sharp contrast with the EU's binding approach — but the voluntary testing window sets a new de facto pre-release norm for frontier labs.

### 5. Colorado AI Act Takes Effect June 30; EU Transparency Rules Arrive in August
**Source**: Wilson Sonsini / EU Digital Strategy | **Category**: Policy

Two major regulatory deadlines are now weeks out. Colorado's AI Act — the most comprehensive US state AI law to date — mandates security risk-management programs, impact assessments, and anti-discrimination measures for high-stakes decisions, effective June 30. The EU's AI Act transparency provisions follow in August, requiring labeled AI-generated content and compulsory Code of Practice adherence. Companies that deferred compliance work are running out of runway.
