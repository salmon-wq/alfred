**Source**: Baker Botts / Wilson Sonsini | **Category**: Policy

Colorado's AI Act becomes enforceable on June 30, two days from now. The law imposes security risk-management programs, algorithmic impact assessments, and anti-discrimination measures on any company deploying AI for "consequential decisions" — lending, hiring, housing, healthcare, and legal services. Colorado joins California in creating a state-level compliance layer that fills the gap left by Congress's continued inaction on federal AI legislation. Companies without documented impact assessments will be exposed to enforcement from Monday.
