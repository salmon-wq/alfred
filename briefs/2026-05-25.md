### 4. EU AI Act Gets First Amendments: Deadline Deferrals and Two New Prohibitions
**Source**: Inside Privacy | **Category**: Policy

EU negotiators reached a provisional agreement on the Digital Omnibus on AI — the first amendments to the EU AI Act since its June 2024 adoption. High-Risk AI Systems compliance obligations are deferred from August 2, 2026 to December 2, 2027, easing near-term pressure on enterprises. Two new prohibited practices are added: AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery and AI-generated CSAM. The timeline relief will be welcomed by industry, but the new prohibitions signal the EU's continued willingness to add hard limits as harms manifest.
