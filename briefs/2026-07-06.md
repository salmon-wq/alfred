### 6. EU AI Act Reaches Full Applicability on August 2
**Source**: European Commission | **Category**: Policy

The EU AI Act enters full applicability on August 2, 2026 — less than four weeks away. Enterprises using high-risk AI systems face mandatory conformity assessments, transparency obligations, and human oversight requirements. Prohibited-use provisions and AI literacy obligations have been in force since February 2025; the August date covers the broader high-risk tier. Companies without compliance programs already underway are now in crisis mode.
