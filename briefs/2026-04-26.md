### 2. Anthropic Builds Claude Mythos 5 — Then Decides Not to Ship It
**Source**: Kersai / devFlokers | **Category**: Models / Safety

Mythos 5 excels at cybersecurity, long-range planning, and academic research, and reportedly exposed "chunk-skipping" failure modes in prior models at scale. The decision lands as a concrete safety-over-deployment precedent that other labs will be measured against.

### 4. White House Pushes Federal AI Preemption of State Laws
**Source**: Consumer Finance Monitor / Cooley | **Category**: Policy

The White House's National AI Policy Framework, released March 20, explicitly recommends that Congress preempt state AI laws that "impose undue burdens," aiming to replace the emerging patchwork of fifty state regimes with a single national standard. California's Gov. Newsom responded on March 30 with Executive Order N-5-26, directing state agencies to draft their own AI safety requirements for contractors — a direct counter-posture. Colorado delayed its own AI law to June 30, and the EU is pushing key AI Act compliance deadlines to 2027–2028. The preemption debate is now the defining governance tension of the year.
