### 4. White House AI task force urges easing liability to encourage AI incident disclosure
**Source**: Bloomberg | **Category**: Policy

Officials on the administration's AI task force recommended that agencies relax cyber-liability rules so AI firms are more willing to report critical incidents. Whether the next Congress keeps or reverses a "disclose without penalty" approach will shape how much the public learns about AI failures.

### 5. Japan weighs binding orders and penalties for advanced AI developers
**Source**: Crypto Briefing | **Category**: Policy

A Japanese government working group discussed binding orders and legal penalties for developers of advanced AI at a closed-door meeting on November 11. Japan has been one of the most permissive major jurisdictions, so this is a notable change as the EU AI Act's December 2 deadlines approach.
