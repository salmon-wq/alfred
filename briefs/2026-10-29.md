## Top Stories

### 1. GitHub Universe 2026 reframes Copilot as an agent platform for the whole delivery cycle
**Source**: DEV Community / Crypto Briefing / Blockchain.News (GitHub Universe keynote, Oct 28) | **Category**: Industry

GitHub opened Universe 2026 in San Francisco with a Copilot app positioned as one surface from issue to merge, multi-agent Projects that run parallel agent sessions across repositories, and Copilot Fusion, which routes each task to one or more models and is reported to be generally available after a preview as "HydraFusion". Cloud agent sessions can now move between the app, VS Code, the CLI and the web, with Microsoft Foundry handling agent hosting and governance. Day 2 runs today; the efficiency claims (such as roughly 30% cheaper frontier-model use) are GitHub's own and not independently tested.

### 2. AMD to invest $1B in Meta's equity offering as Helios racks go live
**Source**: Stocktwits / Quiver Quant / AMD press release (Oct 27) | **Category**: Industry

AMD will put $1 billion into Meta's stock offering, following Nvidia's $3 billion commitment, and says its Helios rack-scale systems are installed at Meta and in testing ahead of wider deployment. The deal builds on a February 2026 agreement for up to 6 GW of Instinct GPUs and EPYC "Venice" CPUs. Chip suppliers taking equity in their largest customer deepens the partnership, but it also draws fresh concern about circular financing in AI infrastructure.

### 3. Oracle to offer Google TPUs on OCI from 2027
**Source**: Oracle press release / DatacenterDynamics (Oct 27) | **Category**: Industry

Oracle announced that Google's TPUs will be available on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, the first time they will be offered on a hyperscale cloud outside Google, with a phased rollout starting in 2027. Oracle declined to give the size of the first deployment. It is also extending its Nvidia partnership toward multi-gigawatt Vera Rubin capacity, so the move broadens its supply rather than replacing Nvidia.

### 4. Google DeepMind previews Genie 4, a real-time world model with native audio
**Source**: Digg / TechJack Solutions / Unite.AI (Oct 27) | **Category**: Models

Genie 4 generates interactive environments that users can edit mid-simulation in natural language (weather, camera, new characters), with Gemini adapting prompts to the scene. DeepMind calls it its first real-time world model to generate audio natively, and says character simulation is the biggest gain. Access is limited to trusted testers, with AI Ultra subscribers and developer partners promised later; DeepMind itself warns that object permanence drifts over long sessions.

### 5. Liquid AI releases LFM3, six open-weight ~2B models for on-device use
**Source**: MarkTechPost (Oct 27) | **Category**: Models

The LFM3 family covers text, vision, audio, omni and "decision" tasks on a shared base. The text model, LFM3-1.7B, reportedly beats the previous LFM2.5-2.6B with 35% fewer parameters, has a 256K-token context window and fits in about 1 GB at 4-bit. Weights are on Hugging Face and every model can be fine-tuned on a single GPU, but benchmark figures so far are Liquid's own.

### 6. Family files wrongful-death suit against OpenAI over drug-combination advice
**Source**: Scripps local news network (News 5 Cleveland, WPTV, et al.) | **Category**: Policy

The mother and stepfather of 19-year-old Sam Nelson sued OpenAI in California this week, alleging ChatGPT advised him on how to "safely" combine drugs before a fatal overdose. The complaint points to OpenAI's earlier safety claims for GPT-4o and to its recent ChatGPT Health launch. It adds to a growing set of product-liability cases that will test whether chatbot makers owe users a duty of care on medical questions.

### 7. State AI rules near their effective dates as federal law stays absent
**Source**: IAPP / artificialintelligenceregulations.com | **Category**: Policy

New York's RAISE Act requires large frontier developers to register with the state starting in November, Colorado's attorney general held an October 26 hearing on revised rules for its narrower replacement AI law (effective January 1, 2027), and the EU has pushed high-risk AI Act obligations to December 2027. With no comprehensive US federal statute, compliance is being set state by state while the White House presses Congress to pre-empt those laws.

### 8. Late-October funding: ASI at $3B, Pathos AI $237M, Maxinsights $30M seed

ASI, a frontier AI company focused on critical infrastructure and national security, announced a $325 million Series C at a $3 billion valuation. Capital continues to concentrate in defense, biotech and robotics-adjacent AI rather than general-purpose chat.
