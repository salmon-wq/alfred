## Top Stories

### 1. METR: AI is roughly doubling the pace of Anthropic's model progress
**Source**: METR / AP (via WCAX, AJC) | **Category**: Research

Following an embedded review from Aug 12 to Oct 29, METR estimates that Anthropic's internal capabilities index has been improving about 1.9x faster since October 2025 (95% CI 1.5x–2.8x). Its best guess attributes roughly 80% of the speed-up to AI being used in Anthropic's own research. This is the most concrete external evidence yet of AI-driven R&D acceleration at a frontier lab. The caveats are material: Anthropic redacted much of the evidence METR found most informative and cancelled a planned compute-verification component. Anthropic says its own analysis will follow in the coming weeks.

### 2. Justice Department launches a "Super Intelligence Task Force"
**Source**: Nextgov, Decrypt, MLex | **Category**: Policy

The DOJ announced DOJ-SITF on Nov 5. It will coordinate criminal and civil enforcement involving advanced AI, set prosecution priorities, and absorb the AI Litigation Task Force created in January. It targets misuse by foreign governments and transnational criminal groups, and leadership has also framed it as tightening oversight of AI companies. The task force will serve as DOJ's point of contact for the White House AI task force led by DNI Jay Clayton.

### 3. Meta and OpenAI buy multi-agent coding tooling on the same day
**Source**: Axios, OpenAI, SiliconANGLE | **Category**: Industry

On Nov 4, Meta acquired Conductor, an app that runs several coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor) in parallel, and OpenAI announced an agreement to acquire Pierre Computer Company, whose Trees and Diffs libraries already power Codex. Terms were not disclosed for either deal. Conductor's cloud service shuts down Nov 20. Both labs are consolidating the orchestration and code-review layer that sits above the model.

### 4. OpenAI ships Images 2.6, adding GIF creation to ChatGPT
**Source**: The Next Web, Decrypt, Digit | **Category**: Models

Images 2.6 rolled out on Nov 4 across ChatGPT and Codex with animated GIF generation, frame-level editing, and generation that OpenAI says is about 50% faster. The API offers two variants, GPT-Image-2.6 Flare and Sunburst, though GIF support in the API remains unconfirmed. OpenAI reports more than 4 billion images generated per week, up from 3 billion in September.

### 5. Democrats prepare a wave of AI bills ahead of taking the House
**Source**: Claims Journal (Bloomberg), Axios | **Category**: Policy

After the midterms, House Democrats are drafting an AI agenda that includes independent safety testing of frontier models and clearer liability for AI harms. This probably overtakes the bipartisan Trahan–Obernolte frontier bill as the main legislative vehicle. That bill drew bipartisan opposition from more than 200 state legislators over its three-year preemption of state AI laws, so the fight over federal preemption is likely to come back in the next Congress.

### 6. Palo Alto Networks reportedly in talks to buy Clutch Security for ~$500M
**Source**: The Information (via AI Weekly) | **Category**: Industry

The cybersecurity giant is reportedly negotiating to acquire Clutch, an Israeli startup that manages the credentials AI agents use to reach enterprise systems. It would be Palo Alto's third roughly $500M AI-related purchase in about 18 months, after Protect AI and Console. Neither company has confirmed the deal. Securing non-human identities is becoming a category of its own as agent deployments grow.
