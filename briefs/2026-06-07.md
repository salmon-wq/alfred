### 4. White House Issues AI Innovation Executive Order with Voluntary Safety Reviews
**Source**: White House / NPR | **Category**: Policy

On June 2, the White House signed an executive order framing AI as a domain to be "unleashed" rather than regulated, but included one concrete safety mechanism: AI companies are asked to voluntarily submit their most powerful models for government review up to 30 days before public release. The order names OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google explicitly. With no enforcement mechanism, compliance is discretionary — a contrast with the EU AI Act, which reaches full applicability on August 2 and carries binding obligations on high-risk systems.
