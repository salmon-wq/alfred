### 4. OpenAI: Agents Now Complete 46% of 2–4 Hour Internal Research Tasks
**Source**: OpenAI ("Research acceleration: The jagged frontier") / The New Stack | **Category**: Research

OpenAI's November 18 post says agents' estimated success on research tasks that take a human 2–4 hours rose from 23% in March to 46% in October. On 4–8 hour tasks it rose from 13% to 35%. Results vary widely by task type: 79% for monitoring training runs, 35% for launching them, and 25% for inference and serving code. The median researcher's agent usage reached $2,400 a day at list prices. These are OpenAI's own internal numbers, and OpenAI says its method and the mix of delegated tasks changed during the period. The post says: "The agents are not taking over AI research."

### 5. Amazon Reportedly Nears a ~$7B Deal for Decart

Neither company has confirmed. Nvidia's reported $500M+ Inception deal is also still unconfirmed.
