### 1. Anthropic Closes Record IPO With Underwriters' Option Fully Exercised
**Source**: TechCrunch / Boston Globe / company statement | **Category**: Industry

Anthropic announced the closing of its Class A IPO, selling 1.127 billion shares at $83.00 including the underwriters' full over-allotment option. ### 3. US Data-Center Export License Rule Takes Effect
**Source**: OriginBrief / The Collectives | **Category**: Policy

A Commerce Department rule now requires a US license for data centers anywhere in the world that supply large amounts of advanced compute to companies headquartered in China and other restricted countries. Export controls had covered chip shipments; this rule extends them to remote access to compute, closing the cloud loophole that Chinese labs have relied on.

### 4. States and Westminster Push Binding Safety Rules for Frontier Models
**Source**: San José Inside / PYMNTS / OriginBrief | **Category**: Policy

California Gov. Newsom proposed letting state officials order an emergency shutdown of dangerous AI systems, alongside independent audits and whistleblower protections. New York's attorney general stood up an enforcement unit ahead of the RAISE Act taking effect January 1, and the National Governors Association asked Congress for a shared national baseline. In the UK, a Commons select committee recommended an AI Safety and Security Bill with a statutory regulator separate from the AI Security Institute.
