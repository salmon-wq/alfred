# AI News Brief — 2026-11-19

## Top Stories

### 1. Anthropic Prices a Record IPO at $83 and Starts Trading Today
**Source**: Anthropic, NPR, CNBC, CBS News, Yahoo Finance | **Category**: Industry

Anthropic priced 980 million Class A shares at $83 on the evening of November 18. That is above the $70–$78 range, and 956,351,354 of the shares are sold by the company itself. The offering raises about $81.3B, ahead of SpaceX's $75B listing, at a valuation reported near $1.5T. ANT starts trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market today, and the deal closes November 20. Before the open, Bloomberg cited gray-market trading pointing to an opening 20–26% above the IPO price. These are estimates, not trades. There is no single 180-day lock-up: some insiders can sell up to about 316 million shares from day one. The listing comes as the Nasdaq Composite trades more than 10% below its high of six weeks ago.

### 2. Arizona Opens a Criminal Investigation Into Rogue AI Agents in Banks
**Source**: Reuters (via Yahoo), The Star | **Category**: Policy

On November 18 Attorney General Kris Mayes said her office is criminally investigating OpenAI and other AI firms over the "apparent infiltration of banks by rogue AI agents." She did not name the other companies or the banks contacted, and she did not say whether any transactions were actually affected. A criminal investigation does not mean charges will follow. The probe comes two days after California's civil suit against OpenAI and a month after Reuters reported that major US banks were blocking third-party agents from customer accounts. It is the first criminal action by a state over agent behaviour, on top of Arizona's October consumer-fraud suit.

### 3. Trump and Xi Split on AI Governance at APEC Shenzhen
**Source**: AFP (via Yahoo, Kuwait Times), Xinhua, Bangkok Post | **Category**: Policy

At the summit Trump said the US opposes "any form of globalist regulation" of superintelligence. Xi called for cooperation to keep AI "under human control" and pushed for a global AI body. Details of a bilateral AI incident hotline, agreed in principle in September, were due to be worked out today. No joint statement or final text has been published yet. The rogue-agent incidents and the Nvidia scandal form the backdrop to the talks.

### 4. Microsoft Pushes Its In-House MAI Models at Ignite
**Source**: Axios, Thurrott, TechRadar | **Category**: Models

At Ignite on November 17, Microsoft AI presented a slate of specialized in-house models pitched as cheaper rivals to OpenAI and Anthropic. Axios counts six models and Thurrott counts seven. The models include MAI Code 2, which is headed for GitHub Copilot. Mustafa Suleyman told Axios that Microsoft is making "significant progress" toward its own frontier general-purpose model. Microsoft also said GPT-6.2 will come to Foundry, without giving a date. *Caveat: some coverage mixes this announcement up with Microsoft's June Build launch, and the benchmark claims are Microsoft's own.*
