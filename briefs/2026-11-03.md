### 1. Anthropic's amended S-1 warns it may soon hit its self-improvement threshold
**Source**: Dataconomy / DataStudios / BigGo Finance (reporting on the S-1/A filed Nov 2) | **Category**: Safety

Anthropic's amended IPO filing, dated November 2, adds a risk disclosure. It says internal forecasts suggest the company "may imminently" meet its Responsible Scaling Policy definition of "dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI." The filing says no current model has crossed that line. It also says Anthropic would not expect to let Claude design successor frontier models safely without meaningful human supervision, and that it is weighing changes to the policy to keep humans in control. Coverage notes the threshold was narrowed in a July revision to the onset of recursive self-improvement, and that the direction of any further change is not disclosed. It is unusual for a frontier lab to put a self-improvement warning in a securities filing, where misstatements carry legal liability. ### 2. US midterms today: data-center moratoria on the ballot
**Source**: Newsweek / The Week / Bloomberg Government | **Category**: Policy

Voters go to the polls today with data-center construction now a mainstream campaign issue. Governor candidates in at least a dozen states back moratoria, according to Newsweek. New York already has a one-year statewide pause, enacted in July, and Texas paused new approvals in August. At least eight local ballot measures tied to data-center development are being decided today. A May Gallup poll found 71% of Americans oppose an AI data center in their own community. Results in Ohio, Texas, Michigan and Wisconsin will show whether resistance to local buildouts turns into a governing mandate. Recent reporting says AI firms delayed high-yield debt sales until the results are in.

### 3. Agility Robotics SPAC approved; Nasdaq debut set for November 5
**Source**: SEC Form 8-K (Churchill Capital Corp XI) / Pulse 2.0 | **Category**: Industry

Churchill Capital Corp XI shareholders approved the merger with Nvidia-backed humanoid maker Agility Robotics on November 2. Over 84% of votes cast were in favour, and holders of about 44.93% of the voting power attended, per the 8-K. The deal values Agility at $2.5B pre-money, with closing targeted for November 4 and trading as AGLT from November 5. Redemption levels, and so the cash Agility actually receives, have not been reported yet. This would be the first pure-play humanoid robotics listing in the current cycle.

### 4. Kimi K3.1 Pro open weights due tomorrow, with no license announced
**Source**: Let's Data Science / VKTR / Artificial Analysis | **Category**: Models

Moonshot has said it will publish the weights of Kimi K3.1 Pro by November 4. The model launched via API on October 23: a reported 2.8T-parameter mixture-of-experts with 104B active parameters and a 1M-token context, priced at $2/$10 per million tokens. Moonshot says it removed cybersecurity data from training so the open release would not "extend the absolute frontier of cyber capability." No license has been named, and as of the latest coverage nothing had been posted. If it ships as described, it would be the strongest open-weight model available. It would also be the first frontier-scale open release to cite deliberate capability removal as its safety case.

### 5. Claude watermark reportedly extends to older models today, but sources conflict
**Source**: Pasquale Pillitteri / tutkit.com / IT Brief | **Category**: Policy

One report says that from today Anthropic applies its statistical text watermark to Claude Mythos 5, Opus 4.7, Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 on the Claude Platform, with Bedrock to follow a few days later. No primary Anthropic document confirming today's date was found. Treat it as unconfirmed.
