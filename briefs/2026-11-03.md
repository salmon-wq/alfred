### 1. Anthropic's amended S-1 reports $33.2B in nine-month revenue and ~$895B in compute commitments

Anthropic's revised IPO filing reports $33.2B in revenue for the nine months ending September 30, 2026, including about $17B in Q3 alone (+46% from Q2). ### 2. Marvell agrees to buy Lumentum in a ~$115B all-stock deal
**Source**: SEC Form 8-K / 425 filings; Bloomberg | **Category**: Industry

Announced November 2: Lumentum holders receive 4.5 Marvell shares per share, an implied premium of about 23.9%. Lumentum holders would own about 33% of the combined company. Marvell is pairing its networking chips with Lumentum's lasers and optical transceivers as optical links become the bottleneck in AI data centers, and it cites more than $1B in run-rate cost synergies. Both stocks fell, a sign investors are getting wary of high prices for AI-infrastructure deals.

### 3. Google's Gemini 4 Carbon rollout begins with cyber defenders, with developer access expected in 1–2 weeks
**Source**: Khaleej Times / BetaNews / Google | **Category**: Models

Gemini 4 Carbon was announced October 28. Members of Google's cyber-defense program get access first, with general developer access to follow. Introductory pricing is reported at $2/$10 per million tokens. ### 5. Character.ai bankruptcy: DOJ watchdog asks for a privacy ombudsman before user data is sold
**Source**: CNN / Bloomberg Law / CBS News | **Category**: Policy

Character.ai filed for Chapter 11 in Delaware in mid-October, with Disney as stalking-horse bidder at $20M for an IP license. On October 27 the U.S. Trustee asked the court to appoint a consumer privacy ombudsman before any sale. The objection cites sensitive data from minors and health-related data across ~12M monthly users. The ruling will help decide what happens to chat logs when an AI companion company fails.
