### 2. Trump announces an "AI Force" and a forthcoming AI czar
**Source**: Newsweek / The Guardian | **Category**: Policy

The White House unveiled plans for an "AI Force" modeled on Space Force, reporting to a new AI czar yet to be named, tasked with monitoring the sector and staying ahead of Chinese capability. The administration paired the announcement with its existing position that current civil and criminal law is sufficient to deter AI misuse — no new AI-specific statute. Critics note the gap it leaves: agentic systems that act independently still carry no requirement that developers retain the ability to intervene.

### 3. US proposes a bilateral AI safety notification mechanism to Beijing
**Source**: CNN Business / Al Jazeera | **Category**: Policy

Trump and Xi meet in Washington on 24-25 September, with AI, trade, Taiwan and rare earths on the agenda.
