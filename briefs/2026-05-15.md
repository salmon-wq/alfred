### 2. Anthropic's Mythos: Too Dangerous for Open Rollout
**Source**: Axios / CNBC / Just Security | **Category**: Models / Policy

Anthropic's Mythos Preview, a frontier model with exceptional vulnerability-discovery capabilities, launched in April with access limited to roughly 50 vetted partners under Project Glasswing. This week the White House blocked a plan to extend access to 70 additional organizations, and the EU has yet to secure access — in contrast to OpenAI, which offered the bloc access to its GPT-5.5-Cyber model. ### 3. Trump Administration Pivots to AI Oversight
**Source**: Fortune / The Register / Federal News Network | **Category**: Policy

In a sharp reversal from its earlier deregulatory posture, the Trump administration is actively studying an executive order that would require AI models to pass safety evaluations before public release — a framework likened to FDA drug approval. The pivot is driven by Mythos's demonstrated cyber capabilities. Internally, the administration is split over whether U.S. intelligence agencies should lead model evaluation; Commerce and CAISI have already signed pre-deployment testing agreements with Google DeepMind, Microsoft, and xAI.

### 6. OpenAI Shuts Down Sora Six Months After Launch
**Source**: AI industry sources | **Category**: Industry

OpenAI is discontinuing Sora, its AI video-generation product, just six months after public launch. Despite reaching one million downloads in its opening week, active users collapsed to under 500,000 while the service burned an estimated $15 million per day in compute costs. The shutdown is the highest-profile AI product casualty of the current cycle and a clear signal that compute economics, not user acquisition, remain the binding constraint for generative media at scale.
