### 4. EU AI Act Full Applicability Arrives August 2 — With a Partial Reprieve
**Source**: Holland & Knight / Gibson Dunn / Kennedy's Law | **Category**: Policy

The EU AI Act becomes fully applicable on August 2, 2026 — 31 days from today — requiring conformity assessments, technical documentation, CE marking, and EU database registration for high-risk AI systems. However, a Digital Omnibus provisional agreement defers high-risk obligations for stand-alone Annex III systems to December 2027 and for AI embedded in regulated products to August 2028. Article 50 transparency obligations (disclosures for AI-generated content, chatbots, emotion recognition) remain on the original schedule. US enterprises with EU exposure face the August 2 deadline for those transparency provisions regardless of the Omnibus deferral.

**Source**: White House / Holland & Knight | **Category**: Policy

The Trump administration's June 2026 executive order "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security" creates a voluntary framework under which frontier model developers may provide federal government access for cybersecurity and national security assessment before public release. Framed as enabling innovation rather than constraining it, the order nonetheless formalises a channel through which government influence over release timing — as demonstrated with both Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 — can operate.
