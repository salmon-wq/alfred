### 1. Google launches a "universal" Gemini agent for work
**Source**: Bloomberg, Android Headlines, AI Weekly | **Category**: Industry

At Gemini at Work 2026 on October 8, Google unveiled a single enterprise agent that takes objectives rather than step-by-step instructions. ### 2. Anthropic launches the Cyber Mission for critical infrastructure and open source
**Source**: Anthropic Newsroom | **Category**: Policy / Industry

On October 8, Anthropic launched a Critical Infrastructure Defense Program with 11 founding partners, including CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, Dragos, Rockwell Automation and Accenture. Anthropic expects a true-positive rate above 90%, though the reports are not human-reviewed. The bet is that finding bugs has become cheap, while verifying and patching them is now the bottleneck.
