### 3. OpenAI Ships GPT-5.5 Instant as ChatGPT's New Default
**Source**: OpenAI / TechCrunch | **Category**: Models

OpenAI replaced GPT-5.3 Instant with GPT-5.5 Instant as the default ChatGPT model on May 5, reporting 52.5% fewer hallucinated claims on high-stakes prompts covering medicine, law, and finance. The release continues OpenAI's push toward a unified "super app" — a single assistant that spans research, coding, document creation, and multi-step task execution.
