### 3. India Constitutes AI Governance War Room
**Source**: Asanify AI News Digest | **Category**: Policy

India's Union IT Ministry stood up the AI Governance and Economic Group (AIGEG) today — a high-level inter-ministerial body explicitly mandated to map which job profiles and geographies face first-order AI displacement and develop transition plans. Unlike most AI governance bodies focused on safety or bias, AIGEG operates with a labour-market lens from the outset. As the world's largest English-speaking workforce, India's proactive posture marks a shift from passive observer to active manager of AI's economic disruption — a governance template peers are likely to watch.
