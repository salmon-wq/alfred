## Top Stories

### 1. Alphabet Loses ~$500B in Worst Day on Record as AI Mode Earns Less
**Source**: Bloomberg / The Next Web / Yahoo Finance | **Category**: Industry

Alphabet shares fell roughly 12.6–13% on Oct 29 after Q3 results: adjusted EPS of $2.67 missed the $3.02 consensus, free cash flow was negative for a second straight quarter, and search ad growth hit a five-quarter low despite revenue up 25% to $127.6B. Chief business officer Philipp Schindler attributed the slowdown partly to "lower monetization" of AI Mode queries — the clearest admission yet that generative search cannibalises the ad engine that funds it.

### 2. Mistral Releases Open Weights for Trillion-Parameter Large 4
**Source**: TechCrunch / Fortune | **Category**: Models

Mistral published weights for Mistral Large 4 on Hugging Face and its API, a mixture-of-experts model with ~1T total and ~49B active parameters, three weeks after an Oct 6 preview. Mistral claims it is the best Western open model for agentic coding and cyber defense and uses 47% fewer tokens per task than China's GLM-5.3; those benchmarks are self-reported and not yet independently replicated.
