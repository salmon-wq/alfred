# AI News Brief — 2026-10-31

## Overview
The AI capital cycle hit its first real wobble this week: public markets punished Big Tech capex, OpenAI cut its fundraising target, and Anthropic's IPO filing gave investors something to compare against.

## Top Stories

### 1. OpenAI cuts funding target by $10B to ~$20B at a $1.25T valuation
**Source**: Financial Times (via Reuters, Benzinga, Seeking Alpha) | **Category**: Industry

The FT reported on Oct 30 that OpenAI now wants about $20B at a $1.25T valuation. Last month the target was $30B at roughly $1.4T, and some investors have since reduced their allocations. UAE-backed MGX is expected to lead the round, with Sequoia, Coatue, Founders Fund and a16z also taking part. This is the clearest sign so far that the AI selloff is reaching private valuations, and it comes just as Anthropic heads to the public market.

### 2. Alphabet loses ~$480B in market value in one day after Q3 earnings
**Source**: Google Finance, MarketBeat, Yahoo Finance | **Category**: Industry

Alphabet stock gapped down more than 12% after its Q3 2026 report. The company missed on EPS and its AI capex plans spooked investors. Google Finance puts the loss at about $482B, the biggest single-day drop in Alphabet's history. The stock slipped another 1.4% on Oct 30 to close at $279.84 (GOOGL). Credit markets are showing the same strain: Meta's five-year CDS hit a record after it raised capex guidance and posted negative free cash flow ($2.12B in Q3).

### 3. Anthropic publicly files its S-1 and plans a Nasdaq listing as "ANT"
**Source**: Anthropic newsroom; Yahoo Finance | **Category**: Industry

Anthropic made its IPO registration public on Oct 26, following a confidential filing in June. Share count and price range are not set yet. Reporting on the prospectus highlights very fast revenue growth, very heavy spending, and a net loss through June that is mostly accounting charges. Coverage of the OpenAI round says Anthropic is aiming to list next month at a valuation of at least $1.5T. If that holds, the first frontier-lab IPO would price above OpenAI's latest private mark.

### 4. Mistral releases open weights for Large 4, its 1T-parameter model
**Source**: Mistral AI; The Next Web | **Category**: Models

On Oct 29 Mistral put the weights for Mistral Large 4 ("Le Chonk") on Hugging Face, in FP8 and NVFP4 variants. It is a mixture-of-experts model with about 1T total parameters and roughly 50B active. The preview launched Oct 6, and since then the model was red-teamed with cybersecurity partners and state authorities. Mistral's cyber benchmark claims can now be tested independently. It is the largest open-weight model from a Western lab to date.

### 5. New York signs a youth AI chatbot safety law
**Source**: NY Governor's Office; IAPP | **Category**: Policy

Gov. Hochul signed legislation on Oct 28 that bars companion chatbots offered to children from engaging in 12 prohibited categories of behavior. Per secondary reporting, it also requires age checks. Common Sense Media called it "the strongest kids' AI safety bill in the country." New York passed it despite the September federal executive order on AI, which deepens the split between state and federal approaches.

### 6. State attorneys general step up legal pressure on OpenAI
**Source**: Texas Attorney General; Texas Tribune; Fox 7 Austin | **Category**: Policy

Texas AG Ken Paxton's Oct 23 lawsuit wants OpenAI barred from offering ChatGPT to minors in Texas. It also asks for age verification and an independent evaluator who would review new models before release. It follows suits from eight other states earlier this month, and Florida's AG has separately asked a court to halt OpenAI's new-model development without independent oversight. These are allegations, not rulings, but pre-release review demands are becoming a recurring tool for states.

### 7. Funding continues for vertical AI despite the selloff
**Source**: Tech Startups; StartUp Beat | **Category**: Industry

On Oct 30 Dulo raised a $130M seed round for AI models aimed at engineering and hardware design. Personal-AI startup Wabi reportedly raised $55M at a $1.25B valuation. AI pen-testing firm AvalonX came out of stealth with $10M. Money is still reaching application-layer and domain-specific bets while the frontier labs reprice.
