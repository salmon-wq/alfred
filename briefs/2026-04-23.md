## Top Stories

### 1. OpenAI Pulls the Plug on Sora
**Source**: TechCrunch / ThePlanetTools.ai | **Category**: Industry

OpenAI confirmed Sora, its AI video generator, will close to users on April 26 with the API following on September 24. The product was burning ~$1M/day in compute while peak active users fell below 500K and lifetime in-app revenue reached only $2.1M — an unsustainable ratio. Compute is being redirected to coding tools and enterprise products ahead of a likely IPO, a clear signal that generative media products without enterprise anchors face an existential cost problem.

### 2. Anthropic Builds a Model It Won't Release
**Source**: LLM Stats / Kersai | **Category**: Models / Safety

Anthropic completed Claude Mythos 5 — reportedly 10 trillion parameters, built for advanced cybersecurity and coding — then chose not to deploy it, marking the first public instance of a major frontier lab suppressing a finished model on capability-safety grounds. The decision sets a precedent that could define how labs handle future capability thresholds, and invites immediate scrutiny of what evaluation criteria triggered the call.

### 3. Google DeepMind's Genie 3 Generates Persistent 3D Worlds
**Source**: NextBigFuture / Kersai | **Category**: Research

DeepMind shipped Genie 3, a foundation world model capable of producing minutes-long interactive 3D environments at 720p/24fps — a step-change from Genie 2's 10–20 second clips. Worlds respond to text-based "promptable events" in real time. The practical significance is less about gaming and more about synthetic data generation for robotics and embodied-agent training, a bottleneck that has constrained physical-world AI development.

### 6. White House Releases National AI Policy Framework
**Source**: Holland & Knight / Consumer Finance Monitor | **Category**: Policy

The White House published a National Policy Framework for AI on March 20, recommending Congress preempt conflicting state AI laws in favor of a single national standard, and explicitly rejecting the creation of any new federal AI regulatory body. AI would instead be governed through existing agencies with subject-matter expertise. With Colorado's comprehensive AI law delayed to June 30 and the EU AI Act expected to take full effect in August, the US federal position sets up a direct contrast with both state-level and European approaches.

### 8. Google Shuts Down Doppl Virtual Try-On App
**Source**: Jetstream.blog | **Category**: Industry

Google is closing Doppl, its standalone AI virtual try-on app (launched June 2025), on April 30. The technology is not being retired — it migrates directly into Google Search and Shopping listings. The move is consistent with a broader pattern: Google consolidating standalone AI experiments into its core search and commerce surface rather than sustaining separate consumer apps.
