### 3. OpenAI Releases GPT-5.5-Cyber for Vetted Security Teams
**Source**: OpenAI / CNBC / Axios | **Category**: Models / Industry

On May 7, OpenAI rolled out GPT-5.5-Cyber — a permissively tuned variant of GPT-5.5 with reduced guardrails — to cybersecurity teams enrolled in its Trusted Access for Cyber programme.
