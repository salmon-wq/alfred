### 3. Illinois Signs the AI Safety Measures Act
**Source**: WTTW Chicago News | **Category**: Policy

Governor Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act into law on July 6, making Illinois the third major state (after California and New York) to impose statutory AI safety obligations. The law requires developers to report on the possibility that a model could assist in creating chemical, biological, or nuclear weapons or enable large-scale cyberattacks. Notably, both OpenAI and Anthropic publicly backed the bill, which passed with broad bipartisan support — a meaningful signal that frontier labs are willing to accept state-level reporting mandates rather than fight them.
