# AI News Brief — 2026-10-24

## Overview
The bill for the AI build-out is coming due: Meta's cash flow went negative, OpenAI is reportedly raising again at a valuation above $1 trillion, and agentic AI is turning up as the attacker's tool as often as the product.

## Top Stories

### 1. Meta's AI spending pushes free cash flow negative; stock falls ~7%
**Source**: CNBC / Meta Q3 2026 release | **Category**: Industry

Meta reported Q3 revenue of $64.6B (+26% YoY), but capex of $34.9B outran $32.8B in operating cash flow, leaving free cash flow at roughly −$2.1B. Reports describe this as the company's first negative quarter since its 2012 IPO. It also raised the floor of its 2026 capex guidance to $135–145B. Shares fell nearly 7% on Thursday, its five-year credit default swaps hit record highs, and chip stocks sold off with it. This is the clearest sign yet that investors are pricing in the risk that hyperscaler AI capex will not pay back.

### 2. OpenAI reportedly seeking at least $30B at a valuation above $1 trillion
**Source**: Bloomberg (via Reuters, others) | **Category**: Industry

OpenAI is reportedly raising at least $30B as bridge financing in place of an IPO, which Sam Altman has said will not happen this year. Reported valuations vary: Bloomberg puts it at about $1.4T, and some digests cite $1.25T pre-money. That would be up from the $852B valuation in its March 2026 round. Talks are described as early and the terms may change.

### 3. Microsoft ties ConnectWise supply-chain attack to China-aligned group using an agentic AI framework
**Source**: CyberScoop | **Category**: Policy

Microsoft attributed an attack on ConnectWise customers' Microsoft 365 environments to Storm-0940, a China-state-aligned group. Microsoft says the group automated the intrusion with an agentic AI penetration-testing framework and could reach more than 2,250 tenants. A Microsoft executive called it "one of many" in a wave of AI-driven attacks. Separate reports describe a single actor using AI agents to break into 100+ online-store websites for about $50 each. Both point the same way: agents are making attacks cheaper to run at scale.

### 4. Major banks race to contain third-party AI agents accessing customer accounts
**Source**: Axios | **Category**: Industry

Banks including Morgan Stanley and Bank of America are reportedly working to contain risks from unvetted third-party AI agents that can act on customer accounts. The hard problem is attribution: telling an action the customer intended from one an agent took on its own. The same week, Microsoft introduced "Guardian Agents" to monitor AI agents inside enterprises, so a market for agent oversight is starting to form.

### 5. Anthropic launches Claude Duet, a background agent with approval gates
**Source**: Anthropic (via release trackers) | **Category**: Models

Duet, released as a research preview on October 21, works in the background across connected apps. It must get the user's explicit approval before it deletes, pays, signs or sends anything. Access is by waitlist for US Pro and Max subscribers. Its approval-before-acting design answers the same attribution problem the banks in story 4 are dealing with.

### 6. DeepSeek reportedly closes ~$15B round
**Source**: Caproasia, following Bloomberg's Oct 6 report | **Category**: Industry

A report dated October 24 says DeepSeek has raised about CNY 100B (~$15B). Bloomberg had reported on October 6 that a round of at least CNY 80B was taking shape, with Tencent and CATL among the largest backers. That followed a first external round of about $7.4B in June. Reuters has not independently verified the figures. The round would give China's most closely watched open-weight lab the money to compete on compute with US frontier labs.

### 7. AI token prices down 54% from May peak
**Source**: Silicon Data price index (via newsletter digests) | **Category**: Industry

Silicon Data's index of AI inference prices has reportedly fallen 54% from its May peak, to below $1 per million tokens. Inference prices are falling while builders' capex keeps rising (story 1), which squeezes margins at both the model labs and the clouds that resell their capacity.

## Key Themes
- **The capex reckoning:** negative free cash flow at Meta, another mega-round for OpenAI and falling token prices all raise the same question: who earns a return on the AI infrastructure build-out?
- **Agent accountability:** banks, Microsoft and Anthropic are all working out how to attribute, monitor and gate what autonomous agents do.
- **Agentic offense:** state-aligned and lone attackers are using AI agents to scale intrusions, and defenders are working to keep up.
- **Capital concentration:** about $45B in combined reported raises for OpenAI and DeepSeek keeps frontier AI a contest among a handful of very well-funded labs.
