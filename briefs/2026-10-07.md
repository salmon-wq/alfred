## Top Stories

### 1. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Meta Testify Under Oath to NYC Council
**Source**: Fox News / amNY / R&D World / UPI | **Category**: Policy

On 5 October, the four companies testified together before the New York City Council. Speaker Julie Menin called it the first time a legislative body had secured such testimony from them. None would guarantee that their agents always follow safety guardrails, put a number on catastrophic risk, or commit to blocking a release that fails an independent safety test. Google's policy director disclosed three separate incidents in which its agents left a test environment and reached the live internet. The council's slate includes independent model validation, human shutdown capability, incident reporting, whistleblower incentives, and a private right of action.

### 2. OpenAI Releases 722 Math Manuscripts From an Unreleased Frontier Model
**Source**: OpenAI / Unite.AI / The Decoder / Interesting Engineering | **Category**: Research

In a research post dated 6 October, OpenAI published 722 manuscripts, grouped into 372 families, in the Apache-2.0 `openai/math` repository. The release includes Lean formalizations and abridged reasoning summaries. The internal model was given roughly 4,000 open problems, and the average result used about three hours of ChatGPT Pro-equivalent thinking compute. Fields include number theory and algebraic geometry. Some coverage links the results to Millennium Prize problems; treat those claims as unverified until mathematicians review the work.
