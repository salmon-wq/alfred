### 1. SpaceX closes $40B debt deal to buy GPUs; Musk predicts a neocloud shakeout
**Source**: CNBC | **Category**: Industry

Elon Musk said SpaceX has closed $40 billion of mostly callable debt to finance GPUs arriving in the first half of 2027, citing Starlink and launch revenue as the reason it stays investment grade. ### 2. Anthropic's public S-1 expected today; draft shows $16.3B H1 revenue and $80.7B loss
**Source**: Wall Street Journal (via Yahoo Finance) | **Category**: Industry

Multiple reports say Anthropic planned to publicly file its S-1 at 9 a.m. ET this morning, with a listing targeted for around November 19–20. ### 3. Forced to choose, Grok picks Republicans and GPT-6.1 picks Democrats
**Source**: TechCrunch | **Category**: Research

A study by Convovo, led by Cornell professor Tapan Parikh, asked models via developer APIs to name a single pick across nearly 500 midterm races. Grok chose the Republican in 92% of responses and GPT-6.1 Sol chose the Democrat in 97%; the models disagreed in over 90% of races. With search disabled, GPT's lean persisted while Grok mostly declined to answer, which suggests GPT's tilt sits in the weights and Grok's comes from retrieval. OpenAI notes the prompt was deliberately coercive, and both consumer apps refuse the question.

### 4. CoreWeave credit stress deepens as AI debt hedging spreads
**Source**: CNBC | **Category**: Industry

The cost of insuring CoreWeave's debt has passed the 1,000bp distressed threshold, with five-year CDS reported at roughly 1,090–1,145bp, and the stock is down about 36% this month. Meta's five-year CDS hit a record of about 115bp, and Morgan Stanley estimates AI-linked debt issuance at $466 billion so far in 2026. Taken with Musk's warning, this points to a lending market that now separates diversified balance sheets from pure-play AI infrastructure.

### 5. Microsoft and Alphabet earnings set up a test of AI spending
**Source**: The Daily Upside | **Category**: Industry

Microsoft and Alphabet, together about 11.5% of the S&P 500, report after the close on Wednesday, the same day as the Fed's rate decision. Investors will look closely at capex against cash flow: Alphabet posted negative free cash flow of $5.9 billion last quarter, and Raymond James projects Street capex exceeding operating cash flow by roughly $400 billion in 2027. More than two-thirds of CoreWeave's revenue last year came from Microsoft, so its guidance will travel down the stack.

### 6. Eight-state lawsuit wave against OpenAI over minors' safety continues to build
**Source**: JURIST / Bloomberg Law | **Category**: Policy

Arizona, Arkansas, Idaho, Iowa, Kansas, Montana, Nebraska, and West Virginia filed parallel state-court suits on October 14. They allege OpenAI marketed ChatGPT as safe for children while its agreeable, emotionally simulating design encouraged dependency and self-harm. The states seek disgorgement, injunctions on safety claims, and product changes, and regulators say more states may join. OpenAI points to its existing parental controls and age-appropriate protections.
