## Top Stories

### 1. Anthropic's S-1 reportedly set to go public; draft shows steep revenue ramp and steeper losses
**Source**: WSJ (via Investing.com) / Dealroom / Yahoo Finance / Forkast | **Category**: Industry

Several outlets report the public S-1 is scheduled for release today, following a confidential draft submitted June 1. A WSJ review of the draft cites ~$16.3B in H1 2026 revenue (vs ~$1.1B a year earlier), an $80.7B net loss, and ~20% of revenue from two customers. Anthropic reportedly told investors Q3 revenue reached $16.8B, up 45% quarter-on-quarter. Analysts are focusing on roughly $518B in planned cloud and compute commitments. Timing has slipped before (Reuters pointed to late September), and a public Manifold market on an EDGAR filing by Oct 31 is open. Treat it as unconfirmed until the filing appears on EDGAR.

### 2. OpenAI models and Codex arrive on Google Cloud
**Source**: The Next Web / Techzine / Scalevise | **Category**: Industry

Announced Oct 21: GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6 Luna are in private preview in Google Cloud's Model Garden, with GA "in the coming weeks." Codex CLI and Desktop can route inference through Google's platform. Billing runs through Google Cloud Marketplace and counts against existing commitments. OpenAI is now on all three major US clouds (AWS since April, after Microsoft's exclusivity ended), and Google now hosts Gemini, Claude and GPT side by side. Pricing, regions and specs were not disclosed.

### 3. Earnings week puts AI capex on trial
**Source**: Reuters "Take Five" (via Investing.com) / Tom's Hardware / FourWeekMBA | **Category**: Industry

Alphabet, Amazon and Microsoft report this week, alongside rate decisions from the Fed, ECB and Bank of Japan. ### 5. Campaigns pitch ways to shape chatbot answers to voters
**Source**: Reuters (via TechBooky), Oct 26 | **Category**: Policy

Documents reviewed by Reuters show US campaign consultants pitching services to get candidates into, and potentially influence, AI chatbot answers ahead of the midterms. In effect it is "generative engine optimization" applied to elections, and it pressures model providers' election-integrity policies. This brief relies on secondary coverage of the Reuters report.
