# AI News Brief — 2026-10-22

## Overview
Washington takes centre stage today: Anthropic is shopping a narrow federal AI safety bill, the US government is barring DeepSeek from its agencies, and private money keeps pouring into AI at nine- and ten-figure scale.

## Top Stories

### 1. Anthropic circulates a draft federal AI safety bill with hard intervention thresholds
**Source**: Politico (via Interesting Engineering, PYMNTS) | **Category**: Policy

Anthropic has privately shared a draft bill with Congress. Under it, the government could halt a frontier model only for a foreseeable, imminent risk of more than $1B in damages or more than 50 deaths or serious injuries, and only for bioweapons, a model escaping its developer's control, or cyberattacks. The Commerce Department could not suspend a model on its own authority: it would have to get an order from a federal judge, with registered independent evaluators required to report "imminent enumerated risk" within 72 hours. Critics are focusing on the dollar threshold. The text itself is unpublished, so details come from press accounts.

### 2. US moves to bar DeepSeek from federal agency systems
**Source**: Bloomberg | **Category**: Policy

A Department of Homeland Security notice in the Federal Register (posted 19 October) bars civilian federal agencies from using DeepSeek products and services and orders existing deployments removed. It is the most concrete federal action yet against a Chinese frontier lab, and it is likely to shape contractor procurement rules downstream.

### 3. DeepSeek reportedly closing a round of about $15B
**Source**: Trending Topics (citing press reports) | **Category**: Industry

DeepSeek is reportedly raising about 100B yuan (around $15B) from Boyu Capital, CPE, Legend Capital and existing backer IDG Capital. If it closes, this would be one of the largest private AI raises outside the US. It lands in the same week as the US federal ban, which underlines how far the US and Chinese AI capital stacks have split. The company has not confirmed the round.
