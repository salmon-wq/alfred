### 4. Four nonprofits sue OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and SpaceX's AI unit to freeze frontier training
**Source**: Venture Atlas, Global Banking & Finance Review | **Category**: Policy

Public Citizen, Heat Initiative, the Alliance for Secure AI and the Irreplaceable Education Fund filed in San Francisco Superior Court. They argue under California's Unfair Competition Law that building AI systems known to be unsafe is a public nuisance. They seek an emergency injunction that would hold until the labs can show the technology is under human control. No freeze has been ordered, and none of the defendants had commented when the reports were published. Whatever the outcome, the case tests a legal route around Congress's inaction.

### 5. Anthropic's tightened China access policy takes effect; accounts closed
**Source**: Technology.org | **Category**: Policy

As of November 12, Anthropic's revised Supported Regions Policy bars users in China, companies registered or headquartered there, and organisations controlled, directly or indirectly, from unsupported regions. Users who fail identity checks face account closure, and secondary reports describe suspensions of previously verified accounts. Anthropic has not said how many accounts are affected. The policy turns export-control-style screening into a routine platform rule.
