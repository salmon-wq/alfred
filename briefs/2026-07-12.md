### 5. Illinois Signs Nation's Most Comprehensive State AI Safety Law
**Source**: WTTW Chicago / TechPolicy.Press | **Category**: Policy

Governor JB Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act into law on July 6. The bipartisan legislation requires developers of large-scale AI systems to disclose safety practices, report major incidents, and submit to independent third-party audits by qualified experts with no financial conflicts of interest. Illinois becomes the most aggressive US state regulator, setting a template that other states are expected to follow. At the federal level, the FTC is soliciting public comment through July 31 on a policy statement addressing the legal status of state laws that require alterations to AI model outputs.
