### 2. U.S. Government Forces Emergency Suspension of Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5
**Source**: Al Jazeera, Fortune, Anthropic | **Category**: Policy / Models

On June 12 at 5:21pm ET, the U.S. government issued an emergency directive under national security authority ordering Anthropic to immediately suspend access to Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for all foreign nationals — including its own foreign national employees. The government cited awareness of a jailbreak technique exploiting alleged model vulnerabilities. Anthropic complied but publicly disputed the proportionality of the action, stating it received only "verbal evidence of a potential narrow, non-universal jailbreak" and that suspending models deployed to hundreds of millions of people was a disproportionate response. All other Anthropic models remained accessible.

### 3. White House Signs Executive Order on AI Innovation and Security
**Source**: White House, Holland & Knight | **Category**: Policy

President Trump signed "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security" on June 2 — the most substantive U.S. federal AI policy action of the year to date. Key provisions: a voluntary 30-day pre-release government review framework for frontier models; an AI Cybersecurity Clearinghouse coordinating vulnerability scanning across agencies; accelerated federal cybersecurity hiring; and prioritized prosecution of AI-driven cybercrime. The framework is largely voluntary, which limits its immediate enforcement weight, but it formally integrates frontier AI into U.S. national security architecture.
