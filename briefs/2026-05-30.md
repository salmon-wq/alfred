### 3. Meta Cuts 8,000 Jobs and 6,000 Open Roles to Fund $135B AI Bet
**Source**: NPR / The Next Web / Quartz | **Category**: Industry

Meta began notifying roughly 8,000 employees of layoffs on May 20 — approximately 10% of its workforce — while simultaneously cancelling 6,000 open requisitions, bringing the effective headcount reduction to 14,000. The company is reorganising engineers into AI-focused "pods" and redirecting 7,000 workers into units including Applied AI Engineering and the Agent Transformation Accelerator XFN. The paradox: Meta posted $201B in 2025 revenue (+22% YoY) and is guiding $115–135B in 2026 capex. The layoffs are not about distress; they are about the composition of the workforce, not its cost.

### 6. Mistral Acquires Emmi AI, Brings Physics-Aware Modelling In-House
**Source**: StartupHub.ai | **Category**: Models / Industry

Mistral acquired Emmi AI, a Vienna-based spinout of NXAI that raised the largest Austrian seed round on record. The 30-person team specialises in physics-aware AI — models that embed physical constraints rather than learning them from data. The acquisition is a capability play: as Mistral competes with labs ten times its size on general language tasks, acquiring deep physics expertise gives it a defensible niche in scientific computing and industrial simulation where raw language performance is table stakes.

### 7. EU AI Act Amendments Agreed; Full Applicability Set for August 2026
**Source**: European Commission | **Category**: Policy

EU member states and the European Parliament reached political agreement on May 7 on amendments to simplify implementation of the AI Act ahead of its August 2, 2026 full-applicability date. The amendments ease administrative burden on SMEs while preserving the core transparency and high-risk classification framework. US federal policy is simultaneously moving in the opposite direction, with an executive order pushing agencies toward a "minimally burdensome" framework that explicitly targets preemption of the patchwork of state AI laws now in force in Colorado, California, and New York.
