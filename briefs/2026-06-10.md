## Top Stories

### 1. Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5 (and Mythos 5)
**Source**: TechCrunch / Anthropic | **Category**: Models

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 on June 9, the first publicly available model from its restricted Mythos tier. Fable 5 leads state-of-the-art benchmarks in software engineering, scientific research, vision, and agentic tasks, with safeguards that redirect to Opus 4.8 in high-risk domains (bio, cyber, chemistry) — triggering in under 5% of sessions. ### 2. Apple WWDC: Gemini Powers Siri; iOS 27 Lets Users Pick Claude or Any AI
**Source**: Business Standard / 9to5Mac | **Category**: Industry

At WWDC 2026 on June 8, Apple unveiled a Gemini-powered Siri under a multi-year deal worth approximately $1 billion per year. More consequentially, iOS 27 introduces an "Extensions" framework that lets users swap the AI backbone of Siri and Apple Intelligence to Claude, Gemini, or any supported third-party model — making this the first time Anthropic's Claude becomes a native iPhone option. Gemini queries run through Apple's Private Cloud Compute, so Google gains no access to user data. Developer betas are live; public betas follow in July ahead of a Fall 2026 launch.

### 3. Bipartisan "Great American AI Act" Introduced in Congress
**Source**: McDonald Hopkins / NPR | **Category**: Policy

On June 4, Representatives Jay Obernolte (R-CA) and Lori Trahan (D-MA) released a discussion draft of the Great American Artificial Intelligence Act of 2026 (GAAIA), the first bipartisan attempt at a comprehensive federal AI governance framework. The bill spans four titles: Frontier AI Governance, Workforce, Cybersecurity, and Research & International Cooperation. Days earlier, on June 2, the Trump administration issued an executive order asking AI companies to voluntarily submit their most powerful models for government testing up to 30 days before public release — framing it as security cooperation rather than regulation.

**Source**: Crescendo AI / ICLR 2026 | **Category**: Research
