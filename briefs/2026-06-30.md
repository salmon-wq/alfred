### 1. Colorado AI Consumer Protection Act Takes Effect Today
**Source**: White House / State of Colorado | **Category**: Policy

Colorado's Consumer Protections for Artificial Intelligence Act — the first comprehensive state AI law in the United States — comes into force on June 30, 2026. The law imposes obligations on developers and deployers of "high-risk AI systems," including impact assessments, transparency disclosures, and anti-discrimination requirements. With federal legislation still in draft, Colorado's law sets a de-facto national baseline and will pressure other states and Congress to harmonize or compete.

### 2. Fable 5 & Mythos 5 Still Suspended Under US Export Directive
**Source**: Anthropic | **Category**: Policy / Models

The US government's June 12 national security directive — ordering suspension of Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 access for foreign nationals — remains substantially in effect, marking the first time a government has forced a frontier model off the market mid-deployment. Only Mythos 5 has been partially restored, and only for vetted critical infrastructure defenders. Anthropic has apologized to affected customers but has not given a timeline for full reinstatement. The episode has triggered urgent debate about export-control frameworks for AI and their extraterritorial reach.

### 3. OpenAI Previews GPT-5.6 With Three-Tier Lineup
**Source**: OpenAI | **Category**: Models

OpenAI previewed GPT-5.6 on June 26, restructuring its model family into three named tiers: Sol (flagship, hard reasoning tasks), Terra (balanced everyday performance), and Luna (fast and low-cost for high-volume workloads). The move mirrors competitive pressure from tiered offerings across the industry and positions the company ahead of its widely anticipated Q4 IPO targeting a near-$1 trillion valuation.

### 4. Great American AI Act Discussion Draft Released
**Source**: Representatives Obernolte & Trahan | **Category**: Policy

A bipartisan House discussion draft of the Great American Artificial Intelligence Act (GAAIA) was published on June 4, proposing the first comprehensive federal AI framework in the US. Key provisions: mandatory disclosures from frontier model developers, third-party audits through designated Independent Verification Organizations (IVOs), and whistleblower protections. The draft runs parallel to the EU AI Act and signals that federal pre-emption of Colorado-style state laws is now a live legislative question.

### 5. EU AI Act Amended — High-Risk Obligations Delayed
**Source**: European Parliament | **Category**: Policy

The European Parliament on June 16 granted final approval to material amendments to the EU AI Act, delaying application of several significant obligations on high-risk AI systems and simplifying compliance rules. The European Commission separately published a Code of Practice on labelling AI-generated content on June 10. The amendments reduce near-term compliance burden on deployers but have drawn criticism for weakening the Act's original teeth.
