### 1. AI Money and Data Centers Shape the US Midterms
**Source**: TechCrunch / STAT / Inside AI Policy | **Category**: Policy

AI PACs on both sides spent millions on Tuesday's races. The central fight was whether Congress should pass a national AI framework that overrides state laws. ### 2. Anthropic's IPO Comes Into Focus After Amended S-1
**Source**: NBC News / Yahoo Finance / Memeburn | **Category**: Industry

Anthropic has applied to list on Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT." An amended S-1 dated November 2 adds Q3 figures: reportedly about $16.96B in revenue, roughly 12x a year earlier, against a $23.3B net loss. Pricing and share counts have not been set. Press valuation estimates range from $1.5T to $2T. After OpenAI delayed its own listing, this is the last major 2026 IPO that traders expect.

### 3. China Accelerates Broad AI Legislation
**Source**: Bloomberg | **Category**: Policy

China's National Development and Reform Commission is speeding up legislation that would govern AI across a model's whole life cycle, citing security lapses worldwide. Alibaba, Tencent and Xiaomi have reportedly also signed a national self-regulation agreement covering eight areas of AI agent governance. This continues Beijing's pattern of regulating early and specifically while US federal policy stays unsettled.

### 4. Big Labs Buy Agent-Tooling Startups
**Source**: Axios / The Wall Street Journal (via newsletter roundups) | **Category**: Industry

OpenAI is acquiring Pierre Computer, maker of the Code Storage Git hosting already built into the Codex app. Meta bought Conductor, a Mac app for running coding agents in parallel. CrowdStrike agreed to buy agent-evaluation firm Fiddler AI, reportedly for about $400M (per the WSJ). Salesforce is buying contact-center voice-agent company Cresta. Together these deals show the race moving from model quality to the tools agents need to run.

### 5. Fal Doubles Valuation to $16B
**Source**: Bloomberg | **Category**: Industry

Inference platform Fal, which hosts image, video and audio models for developers, raised a reported $1.3B led by Bessemer at a $16B valuation, double its previous mark. Traversal also raised $120M at a $1B valuation. Newsletter tallies say about 2.5% of deals are taking roughly 65% of venture dollars.

### 6. Meta Reportedly Seeks $20–25B in Bonds as AI Spending Bites
**Source**: News aggregators (single-source, unconfirmed) | **Category**: Industry

Meta is reportedly preparing a $20–25B bond sale because AI infrastructure spending has turned its cash flow negative. If confirmed, this would be a clear sign that even the richest hyperscalers now need debt to pay for AI capex.

### 7. Apple Opens Siri AI Integration to All Developers
**Source**: News aggregators (developer beta) | **Category**: Models

The iOS 27.3 beta and related betas reportedly let all developers integrate with Siri's AI layer in preview. This would widen Apple's agent surface beyond first-party apps ahead of a broader rollout.
