### 1. Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 Suspended by US Government
**Source**: AY Automate / ITECS | **Category**: Policy

The incident crystallises a new risk category for frontier labs: rapid government intervention post-launch.

### 7. Colorado AI Act Takes Effect in 15 Days
**Source**: Wilson Sonsini / Baker Botts | **Category**: Policy

Colorado's AI Act (SB 24-205) — the first US statute comprehensively regulating "high-risk" AI systems — takes effect June 30. Affected companies must complete impact assessments, publish consumer disclosures, and demonstrate reasonable care to prevent algorithmic discrimination across consequential decision domains: lending, healthcare, housing, employment, and legal services. With the White House simultaneously negotiating federal preemption of state AI laws, the Colorado Act may be both the first and last of its kind to actually take effect.

### 8. Google Unveils TurboQuant: KV-Cache Compression for Long-Context Models
**Source**: Crescendo AI / InfoWorld | **Category**: Research

Google's research team presented TurboQuant at ICLR 2026, addressing KV-cache memory overhead — a primary bottleneck for long-context model deployment. The result has direct practical implications for the 1M+ context models from DeepSeek and Anthropic's Opus 4.8 released this week.
