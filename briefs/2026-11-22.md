## Top Stories

### 1. Anthropic Lists on Nasdaq in the Largest IPO on Record
**Source**: CNBC / Fox Business / Yahoo Finance | **Category**: Industry

Anthropic priced at $83 a share, above its $70–78 range, raising about $81.3B. It began trading as ANT on November 19, opening at $111.50 (+34%) and closing near $97.17 (+17%). It is the first frontier AI lab to list publicly, so its quarterly numbers will now act as a public check on AI-lab economics.

Analysts said one strong listing does not answer the sector's concerns about AI profitability, capex, and interest rates. The Nasdaq had already entered correction territory earlier in the week.

### 3. Google Releases Gemini 4 Neon
**Source**: Google Blog / 9to5Google | **Category**: Models

Gemini 4 Neon arrived on November 18 as the mid-tier model between Carbon (the heaviest) and Hydrogen (the fastest). It is priced at $3 / $15 per million input/output tokens with a 1M-token context window. Google says it ranks #3 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, though that is a Google-reported figure. A cyber-defense variant is limited to vetted defenders.

### 4. Governors Ask for Federal Frontier-AI Rules That Leave States Their Authority
**Source**: Bloomberg | **Category**: Policy

A bipartisan group of governors, working through the National Governors Association, wants Washington to regulate the most powerful AI systems while states keep authority over consumer protection, discrimination, and labor impacts. The plan includes independent safety reviews of advanced models and mandatory reporting of serious security breaches. It arrives as the incoming Democratic-controlled Congress reshapes the federal preemption debate.

### 5. House Committee Discloses ~879,000 AI Chips Approved for China
**Source**: The Standard / AI Weekly | **Category**: Policy

House Foreign Affairs Chair Brian Mast used Export Control Reform Act authority to publish license data. Commerce approved more than 879,000 Nvidia H200 and equivalent AMD chips for Chinese firms between December 2025 and May 2026, out of 3.4M requested. Every application rated diversion risk as "low." The disclosure strengthens the case for the AI Overwatch Act, which would give Congress review power over chip export licenses.

### 6. Washington Tightens Controls on Chips and Chinese Models
**Source**: Crypto Briefing / InsideAIPolicy | **Category**: Policy

A Commerce interim final rule now requires licenses to export advanced AI chips to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brazil, which puts Southeast Asia's data-center buildout at risk. Separately, a House committee called for removing Chinese open-weight models from government systems, following a November 18 US-China Commission recommendation to block new deployments unless the models pass independent evaluation.

### 7. Deal Flow: Palo Alto Buys Applied Compute; Nvidia Reportedly Near Inception Deal
**Source**: TechReport / The Information / AI Funding Tracker | **Category**: Industry

Palo Alto Networks agreed to acquire AI infrastructure startup Applied Compute in a $4.5B all-stock deal. The Information reports Nvidia is nearing a purchase of diffusion-model developer Inception for more than $500M (unconfirmed). Cognition is reportedly raising at a $75B valuation. No funding round above $2M closed in the 24 hours to November 21, so recent activity has been mostly talks rather than closed deals.
