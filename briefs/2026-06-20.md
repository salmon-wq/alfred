### 5. Colorado AI Act Takes Effect June 30 — Ten Days Out
**Source**: Wilson Sonsini / Kiteworks | **Category**: Policy

Colorado's AI Act becomes enforceable in ten days, imposing substantive obligations on developers and deployers of high-risk AI systems: algorithmic discrimination assessments, transparency disclosures, and consumer redress mechanisms. It is the most operationally demanding state AI law to date. Simultaneously, the Trump Administration's deregulatory executive order is asserting federal preemption over state AI laws — setting up a legal collision that Colorado's compliance deadline will help crystallise.
