### 3. China's AI Companion Regulation Takes Effect — Doubao and Qwen Shut Down
**Source**: TechTimes / AI Governance Institute | **Category**: Policy

China's Interim Measures for the Administration of AI Anthropomorphic Interactive Services — the world's first dedicated regulatory category for AI companion and personalized agent services — became enforceable on July 15. ByteDance's Doubao and Alibaba's Qwen were required to shut down their personalized AI agent features, affecting hundreds of millions of users. Doubao users have until October 15 to export their data; Qwen offered no migration path. The law covers any service simulating human personality traits and sustained emotional interaction, and drew up companion safety standards that have no direct equivalent anywhere else in the world.

**Source**: Tom's Hardware / FourWeekMBA | **Category**: Models / Policy

Moonshot AI released open weights for Kimi K3 — a 2.8-trillion-parameter model that topped major coding leaderboards — in mid-July, enabling organizations to self-host a top-tier system with no per-token cost. The release has revived a Trump administration push to restrict or ban Chinese AI models on cybersecurity grounds. **Source**: WTTW Chicago / TechPolicy.Press | **Category**: Policy

Illinois joins California and New York in a regulatory bloc that analysts estimate covers roughly 40% of the U.S. AI market.
