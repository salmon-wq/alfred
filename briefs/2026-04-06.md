### 4. Google TurboQuant Addresses KV-Cache Bottleneck at ICLR 2026
**Source**: Crescendo AI / LLM Stats | **Category**: Research

Google Research presented TurboQuant at ICLR 2026 — an algorithm that materially reduces KV-cache memory overhead in large context-window models. The method combines PolarQuant vector rotation with Quantized Johnson-Lindenstrauss compression into a two-step pipeline. KV-cache is currently one of the primary cost and latency bottlenecks for frontier model inference at scale, and TurboQuant is applicable across the industry, not exclusive to Google's infrastructure.

### 5. GSA Draft AI Procurement Clause Draws Industry Alarm
**Source**: Nextgov/FCW / Federal News Network / Gibson Dunn | **Category**: Policy

GSA's draft clause GSAR 552.239-7001 proposes government ownership of all input data and custom AI model developments produced under federal contracts, plus broad rights to use any AI system for "any lawful government purpose." The comment period closed April 3 after an extension from March 20. BSA, TechNet, and other trade groups formally objected, warning the terms will reduce competition and deter leading AI providers from pursuing federal contracts. Final rule language has not been issued; resolution will set a significant precedent.
