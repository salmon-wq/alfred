## Top Stories

### 1. Alphabet posts worst day in its history, losing ~$500B in value
**Source**: CNBC | **Category**: Industry

Alphabet fell 12.6% on Oct 29 after Q3 results showed −$9.07B in free cash flow (analysts expected +$2.7B) and search ad revenue of $63.35B, below estimates. Search also fell below half of total revenue for the first time. Only Nvidia's January 2025 drop has erased more US market value in one day. It is the clearest sign yet that investors want AI capex to show a return.

### 2. OpenAI cuts funding target by $10B to ~$20B
**Source**: Bloomberg / Financial Times | **Category**: Industry

OpenAI lowered its round target to about $20B at a $1.25T valuation, with roughly $11B committed as of Oct 31. Reports cite the AI stock selloff and competition for investor money from Anthropic's coming listing. It is the first public sign of tighter capital for the leading private lab.

### 3. Anthropic files public S-1 for Nasdaq listing as "ANT"
**Source**: Anthropic / CNBC | **Category**: Industry

Anthropic made its S-1 public on Oct 26, after filing confidentially in June. Press reports cite a net loss of $80.7B through June, mostly accounting charges, and a $42B loss in 2025. Share count and price range are not set yet. SEC rules mean the roadshow can start no earlier than about Nov 10.

### 4. Mistral open-sources Large 4 ("Le Chonk"), a 1T-parameter model for cyber defence
**Source**: Mistral AI / TechRepublic | **Category**: Models

Mistral released the weights on Oct 29. The model has about 1.05T total parameters, roughly 52B active, and a 1M-token context window. Mistral reports 79% on CyberGym-E2E and a 96.6% refusal rate on malicious cyber requests. Launch partners include CrowdStrike, Cisco, Cloudflare and ENISA. All benchmark figures come from Mistral, and open weights cannot be recalled once released, which will sharpen the dual-use debate.

### 5. Google's Gemini 4 Carbon tops the leaderboard but is limited to vetted defenders
**Source**: Engadget / Root-Nation | **Category**: Models

Gemini 4 Carbon was announced Oct 28 and is available only to cyber defenders in Google's Fairwind Program. It scores 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, ahead of Claude Opus 5.5 (58) and GPT-6 Astra (57). Google has given no date for wider access. Google's gated release and Mistral's open one are opposite answers to the same question about releasing cyber-capable models.

### 6. CoreWeave default insurance stays above 10% a year
**Source**: Reuters / Markit | **Category**: Industry

CoreWeave's five-year credit default swaps hit a record 1,194.5bp in October and were still at 1,041bp on Oct 27. That implies a high market-estimated chance of default on about $50B of debt. Spreads on Oracle, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta are also at records, though they have risen far less. Credit markets now price AI-infrastructure risk, not just equity markets.

### 7. Chatbot voter guides show errors ahead of US midterms
**Source**: The Washington Post | **Category**: Policy

The Post asked six chatbots for voter guides covering every US county. The answers included a Missouri Senate race that does not exist and candidates who are not on the ballot. It also found AI-generated websites feeding the chatbots unreliable information. Election day is Tuesday, Nov 3.
