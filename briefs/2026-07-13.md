### 1. OpenAI Ships GPT-5.6 Sol with Ultra Subagent Mode

### 4. Illinois Signs AI Safety Measures Act into Law
**Source**: WTTW Chicago News | **Category**: Policy

Governor JB Pritzker signed the Artificial Intelligence Safety Measures Act on July 6, joining California and New York in state-level AI oversight. The law requires AI developers to report when their models could provide "substantial uplift" for mass-casualty weapons or large-scale cyberattacks. It is the most explicit CBRN-uplift reporting obligation yet enacted at the US state level.

### 5. China's Dual AI Regulations Take Effect in July
**Source**: Rimon Law / CAC | **Category**: Policy

Two China regulatory instruments land this month: AI Ethics-Safety Guidelines (effective July 1) establishing nine core principles including "controllability and trustworthiness," and Implementation Opinions on Intelligent Agents (effective July 15) issued jointly by the CAC, NDRC, and MIIT. The agent-specific guidance is the first major jurisdiction to regulate agentic AI behaviour at the regulatory level rather than leaving it to model-safety frameworks.

US venture capital deployed $412.7B in the first half of 2026 — roughly 30% more than all of 2025 — with $355.9B (86%) going to AI companies. The concentration underscores how capital markets have effectively re-oriented around AI infrastructure, frontier labs, and application-layer bets, leaving most other sectors competing for the remaining 14%.

**Source**: ThursdAI / AI Weekly | **Category**: Research / Policy
