### 2. EU Council and Parliament Reach AI Act Omnibus Deal
**Source**: Council of the EU / TechPolicy.Press | **Category**: Policy

On May 7, EU legislators struck a 4:30 a.m. agreement on the AI Omnibus package, substantially rewriting the enforcement calendar for the AI Act. High-risk AI compliance obligations are pushed to December 2027 (biometrics, critical infrastructure, employment, education, law enforcement) and August 2028 (safety-component systems). The deal adds new outright bans on nudification apps and AI-generated child sexual abuse material, extends SME-tier regulatory exemptions to small mid-cap companies, and reduces the grace period for synthetic-content transparency from six months to three. The EU AI Office receives reinforced powers and the governance fragmentation across member states is partially consolidated.
